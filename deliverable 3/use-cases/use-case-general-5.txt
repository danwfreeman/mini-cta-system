As a rider planning a trip, similar to alerts on a line, now show me all alerts that are currently active for a station,
I want to see the mode of the station in my results (train or bus)


mysql> SELECT
       a.alertID,
       a.title,
       a.description,
       a.severity,
       a.startTime,
       a.endTime,
       s.name AS stationName,
       s.mode as mode
     FROM Alert a
     JOIN AlertStation ast ON ast.alertID = a.alertID
     JOIN Station s        ON s.stationID = ast.stationID
     WHERE s.name = 'Jackson'
       AND '2026-01-22 08:00:00' BETWEEN a.startTime AND a.endTime
     ORDER BY a.severity, a.startTime;
