Show me all alerts that are currently active for a line, can be a train or bus line

For a user that is planning a trip, I want to know if there are any alerts I should be aware of on the Line I'm planning ontaking, 
can be a bus line or a train line

mysql> SELECT
       a.alertID,
       a.title,
       a.description,
       a.severity,
       a.startTime,
       a.endTime,
       l.name AS lineName,
       l.mode AS lineMode
     FROM Alert a
     JOIN AlertLine al ON al.alertID = a.alertID
     JOIN Line l       ON l.lineID = al.lineID
     WHERE l.name = '8 Halsted'
       AND '2026-01-23 06:00:00' BETWEEN a.startTime AND a.endTime
     ORDER BY a.severity, a.startTime;
