As an operator I want to see the rank of stations by service frequency - which stations have the most arrivals

notes:
-we want dense rank here as we don't want gaps in the service rank


mysql> WITH station_arrivals AS (
            SELECT
              s.name AS stationName,
              COUNT(*) AS arrivalCount
            FROM TimeTable tt
            JOIN Station s ON s.stationID = tt.stationID
            GROUP BY s.name
          )
          SELECT
            stationName,
            arrivalCount,
            DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY arrivalCount DESC) AS serviceRank
          FROM station_arrivals
          ORDER BY arrivalCount DESC, stationName;
