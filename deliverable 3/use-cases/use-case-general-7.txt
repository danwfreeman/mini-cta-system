7) General Query

As a rider of the system, for a particular line, show me all stations that are accessible and do not have an alert associated with them.

I want to plan a trip and need an elevator at the station, therefore I want to know which stations along my line have a working elevator.

notes:
-stations wtih alertID of NULL are what we want, typically we'd hide the NULL fields in the user interface, shown here for clarity.

mysql> SELECT
       l.name AS lineName,
       sl.stopSequence,
       s.stationID,
       s.name AS stationName,
       s.isAccessible,
       a.alertID,
       a.title AS alertTitle,
       a.severity,
       a.startTime,
       a.endTime
     FROM Line l
     JOIN StationLine sl ON sl.lineID = l.lineID
     JOIN Station s      ON s.stationID = sl.stationID
     LEFT JOIN AlertStation ast ON ast.stationID = s.stationID
     LEFT JOIN Alert a          ON a.alertID = ast.alertID
     WHERE l.name = 'Red Line'
       AND s.isAccessible = TRUE
       AND a.alertID IS NULL
     ORDER BY sl.stopSequence;
