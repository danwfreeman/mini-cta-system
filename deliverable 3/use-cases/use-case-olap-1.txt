1) OLAP

As a user I want to know the mumber of stops on a trip from one the start station to the end station, useful for bus lines

notes:
- we want to demonstrate an OLAP SUM() query, this could also be achived with a regular count(*) qeury

mysql> SELECT
       l.name AS lineName,
       SUM(1)
     FROM Line l
     JOIN StationLine sl_from ON sl_from.lineID = l.lineID
     JOIN StationLine sl_to   ON sl_to.lineID   = l.lineID
     JOIN StationLine sl_mid  ON sl_mid.lineID  = l.lineID
     JOIN Station s_from ON s_from.stationID = sl_from.stationID
     JOIN Station s_to   ON s_to.stationID   = sl_to.stationID
     WHERE l.name = '66 Chicago'
       AND s_from.name = 'Michigan/Chicago'
       AND s_to.name   = 'Shore Drive'
     AND sl_mid.stopSequence >= sl_from.stopSequence
     AND sl_mid.stopSequence <= sl_to.stopSequence;
+------------+--------+
| lineName   | SUM(1) |
+------------+--------+
| 66 Chicago |      4 |
+------------+--------+
1 row in set (0.001 sec)
