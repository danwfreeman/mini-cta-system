As an operator I want to see the average number of stations per line (train or bus) in the system.

I could save these over time and graph them to get an idea of how the system is progressing in adding stations to bus or train lines (more likely bus lines)

WITH stations_per_line AS (
            SELECT
              l.lineID,
              l.name AS lineName,
              l.mode,           -- 'train' or 'bus'
              COUNT(DISTINCT ls.stationID) AS stationCount
            FROM Line l
            JOIN StationLine ls ON ls.lineID = l.lineID
            GROUP BY l.lineID, l.name, l.mode
          )
          SELECT
            mode,
            lineName,
            AVG(stationCount) AS avgStationsPerLine
          FROM stations_per_line
          GROUP BY mode, lineName;
