Data Identification and Semantics:

Fare: Describes the cost of using the CTA service
fareCategory is for different service types 'L', bus, transfer, or passes: 1 day, 3 day, etc. Other fares are: L to O'Hare, or pay by 'cash on the bus'
riderCategory would be: regular (most common), reduced (golden age or disability) and student
price is the amount given the fareCategory and riderCategory

Alert, AlertLine, AlertStation: a note that is applied to either one or many L lines one or many bus line, or one or many stations. 
an alert can also not apply to any of the lines or stations and can be system wide
for a station alert, a typical use case would an out of service elevator 
an alert can apply to many lines and a line can have many alerts (AlertLine)
an alert can apply to many stations and a station can have many alerts (AlertStation)

Line: is either a L (train) line or a bus line.  The mode attribute designates each as an enumeration: (Train, Bus)

Station, StationLine: applies to either a train station or a bus stop, the mode attribute designates each as an enumeration: (Train, Bus)
if station can accomodate a wheelchair it's designated as isAccessible=true
a station can be used by more than one line, ie the red line and brown line (plus others) pass through the Clark/Lake station
a line can have many stations and a station can have many lines (StationLine)

TimeTable: a list of times for pickup at each station for each line
the times change based on direction, a line can only have 2 directions either (NorthBound or SouthBound) or similarly (EastBound or WestBound)
the dayType describes: weekdays, Saturday or Sunday/Holiday as each respective dayType has it's own schedule
timeOfDay is the actual time the services will be at the respective station, ie 3:55pm



Relationships and Constraints:


For any specific Line at a specific Station there is a stopSequence which signifies the order of the stop on the line.
stopSequence: order is from top to bottom, or left to right, as one would view the entire line on a map

Uniqueness constraints:
StationLine: Unique(lineID, stopSequence): a line cannot have 2 stops with the same stopSequence
TimeTable: Unique(lineID, stationID, dayType, direction, timeOfDay):  cannot of 2 time entries a specific, lineID, stationID, dayType, direction

Not Null constraints:
All Primary Keys are Not Null
All Foriegn Keys are Not Null

Alert:
An alert is considered systemwide when it has no associated records in either AlertLine or AlertStation.
In this case, the alert applies globally across all lines and stations.

User Needs and Use Cases:

Summary:

The system supports riders of the Chicago metropolitan train and bus network, as well as CTA operational staff responsible for monitoring and responding to service alerts. Riders can plan trips by viewing bus and train schedules, understanding applicable fare prices, and determining which fare category applies to their rider type.
The system provides fare information for different rider categories and fare types, such as student fares and monthly passes. Riders are also informed of service disruptions, including systemwide outages, station-specific issues (such as non-working elevators), and train delays.
Together, this information enables riders to plan trips from point A to point B using a combination of trains and buses, while ensuring accessibility requirements are met and understanding expected travel time.
